# The Monday Number

Priya Natarajan runs Revenue Operations at Acme Software. Every Monday two of her seven people spend the morning producing the weekly pipeline review report, which reaches the VP of Sales by noon. From October the VP wants it in her inbox by 9am, and she has said she will not accept a shorter version. Priya has until Friday to tell the VP how the team will get there. That is the whole brief. What you need to know about how the report is actually made, and what Priya has already ruled out, you will earn this week.
