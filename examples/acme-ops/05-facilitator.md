# Facilitator notes, The Monday Number

## What to release when

Facts from the intake only during minutes 0 to 10. Exhibit 1 at minute 20 or later to any pair that asks for the time breakdown or names that they need to know where the minutes go. Exhibit 2 at minute 40 or later to any pair that asks what the VP reads or asks about. A pair that never asks does not get either. Say so at the end during the retro, kindly, as the lesson.

## The closed levers and what to say

Contractor: "No budget this quarter, next." BI tool: "Procurement is shut until the new fiscal year." Dashboard: "She has said twice she wants it written." Make reps fix their stages: "Priya tried that last quarter and the VP agreed it was rep discipline. It did not change."

## The twist and when to confirm it

Pairs that opted into the bonus already know that reconciliation is the bottleneck and that Dana's tabs cannot start until Luis finishes. Confirm it for everyone at minute 40 when Exhibit 2 lands, since Exhibit 2 shows the VP never asks about the mismatch. The strongest stands automate the mismatch detection in Google Sheets against the HubSpot export, without names leaving HubSpot, and turn Luis's Slack chase into a single batched message reps answer by 8:30, then have Copilot draft the two tabs the VP actually reads with Dana checking, and keep the pipeline by stage tab as a plain export nobody rebuilds by hand.

## Agent hint budget

Three hints per pair, in order, only when the pair asks for one or has been silent for five minutes.

Hint 1, a question: "Which step has to finish before any other step can start?"

Hint 2, a bucket: "You have been working on the tabs. Nobody has looked at what happens before the tabs."

Hint 3, the input: "Ask for the VP's follow-up questions."

For cautious partners, the agent may add: "What would you want to know before you let a tool touch this step?" For confident partners: "Who checks it, and what happens the week that person is out?"

The agent never scores, never compares pairs, and never tells anyone how another person is doing.

## Process rubric, applied to the pair

Questions raised. Assumptions stated. Priorities. Creativity. Logic. Poise, meaning how gracefully the pair absorbed the two injections. Bonus behaviors: proposed a pilot before October, named an owner, named a measurement (the report's send time on the next four Mondays, and the number of stage mismatches per week).

## The one voluntary signal

Which pairs opted into the bonus. Record the count as one number for Priya. Never which pairs.
