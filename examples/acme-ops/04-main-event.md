# The Monday Number, live session

## The full brief

Priya Natarajan, Director of Revenue Operations at Acme Software, has to tell the VP of Sales by Friday how the weekly pipeline review report will reach the VP's inbox by 9am every Monday from October, at its current length. Today the report reaches the leadership Slack channel by noon. It is produced by Dana and Luis from the HubSpot open deals view in Google Sheets, with reps confirming stage mismatches on Slack. The team has HubSpot, Slack, Google Sheets, and Copilot. The pipeline schema cannot change. No customer name or email leaves HubSpot into an AI tool. There is no contractor budget, no BI tool, and the VP wants a written report. Your pair's job is to tell Priya what to do, as if she is walking into the room at minute ninety.

## Timeline, ninety minutes

Minutes 0 to 10, restate and clarify. Each pair reads its joint stand or its two hypotheses aloud. The facilitator answers clarifying questions with facts from the intake only, and nothing that is in an exhibit.

Minutes 10 to 20, structure. Each pair writes three to five buckets for where the morning goes and where AI might change one of them. Cautious partners: name what you would ask before touching anything. Confident partners: name what would run on its own and who checks it.

Minutes 20 to 40, build, first gate. Exhibit 1 in full is released to any pair that asks for "the time breakdown" or names the inputs they need to know where the minutes go. Pairs work in Google Sheets and Copilot on the step they chose.

Minutes 40 to 55, second gate. Exhibit 2 is released to any pair that asks what the VP actually reads or asks about.

Minute 55, Injection 1.

Minutes 55 to 70, build.

Minute 70, Injection 2.

Minutes 70 to 80, build.

Minutes 80 to 90, stands. Each pair delivers to Priya.

## Exhibit 1, the full time log

| Step | Owner | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Export open deals view from HubSpot | Dana | 10 |
| Find rows where HubSpot stage does not match the stage in the rep's notes | Luis | 30 |
| Message reps on Slack to confirm the right stage | Luis | 75 |
| Wait for replies and update the sheet | Luis | 40 |
| Rebuild summary tab: pipeline by stage | Dana | 45 |
| Rebuild summary tab: expected close this month | Dana | 40 |
| Rebuild summary tab: slipped deals | Dana | 35 |
| Write the commentary paragraph | Luis | 25 |
| Format and post to leadership Slack | Dana | 20 |

Dana cannot start the summary tabs until Luis has finished reconciling. That dependency is why the morning is a morning.

## Exhibit 2, the VP's follow-up questions, last six Mondays

| Week | Question in the Slack thread |
|---|---|
| 1 | Which three deals slipped and why |
| 1 | What is expected close this month versus last month |
| 2 | Which slipped deals are over 50k |
| 3 | Expected close this month, is it up or down from Friday |
| 4 | Which deals slipped from this month into next |
| 5 | Same as week 4 |
| 6 | Expected close this month, and which three slipped |

She has never asked about the pipeline by stage tab. She has never asked about a stage mismatch. Every question is about two of the three tabs.

## Injection 1, minute 55

Priya's message, read aloud: "The VP just asked whether the report can include a one-line forecast for the quarter starting in October. She knows it is extra. She would like it if it is possible." Pairs decide whether to say yes, and what it costs against 9am.

## Injection 2, minute 70

Priya's message, read aloud: "Luis is out the Monday after next. Can Dana run it alone that week?" This is the coordination question. A plan that only works when both people are at their desks is not a plan for 9am.

## What would you do

Each pair, two minutes, to Priya. Open with the one sentence stand. Then: what changes in the Monday process, which step AI does and which step a person checks, who owns it, what you would pilot before October and how you would know it worked, and the one risk you would tell the VP about. No option surveys. A stand.
