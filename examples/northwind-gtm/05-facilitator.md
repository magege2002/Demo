# Facilitator notes, The Sequence Nobody Owns

## What to release when

Intake facts only during minutes 0 to 10. Exhibit 1 at minute 20 or later to a pair that asks for reply rates by sequence or audience. Exhibit 2 at minute 40 or later to a pair that asks what changed on the sending side or why sends were capped. If a pair asks about the sending side before minute 40 because they hold the merged exhibit, release Exhibit 2 early. That is the bonus paying off in the room and it should.

## The closed levers and what to say

New tool: "The CRO said no until this one recovers." More volume: "The deliverability team capped it, and you will find out why." Copywriter: "No budget this quarter."

## The twist and when to confirm it

Two problems. Deliverability on the cold domain explains most of the fall across all six cold sequences. The unowned review step explains why two of them fell furthest and why a savings claim went out. Confirm both at minute 40 when Exhibit 2 lands. The strongest stands do not rewrite all twelve. They warm the cold domain with the deliverability team, pause the two SDR-rewritten sequences pending a tone read and the legal source, make one person the owner of the review step, have Copilot run a first pass against the Notion brand voice page and flag any delivery or savings claim for legal, and pilot on two cold sequences for two weeks before the CRO meeting with reply rate and bounce rate as the two numbers.

## Agent hint budget

Three hints per pair, in order, only when asked or after five silent minutes.

Hint 1, a question: "Which sequences fell, and which did not? What do the ones that did not have in common?"

Hint 2, a bucket: "You have been working on the words. Nobody has asked how the emails get delivered."

Hint 3, the input: "Ask what changed on the sending side."

For cautious partners: "What would you want to know before a sequence you touched goes to a CFO?" For confident partners: "If the review ran on its own, who signs, and what does the signer see?"

The agent never scores, never compares pairs, and never reports on a person.

## Process rubric, applied to the pair

Questions raised. Assumptions stated. Priorities. Creativity. Logic. Poise under the two injections. Bonus: pilot before the CRO meeting, a named owner for the review step, two named measurements (reply rate and bounce rate per sequence per week).

## The one voluntary signal

Which pairs opted into the bonus. One number for Marcus. Never which pairs.
