# Pair bonus

The bonus appears only if both of you choose to combine what you each hold. Nobody has to.

## Merge the exhibit

Put the two halves side by side. Every cold sequence fell by more than half. Every warm and re-engage sequence barely moved. Each half on its own looks like "some sequences got worse," which points to copy. Together, the split is clean: cold sequences to people who have never heard of Northwind collapsed, sequences to people who already engaged did not. Copy did not change across all six cold sequences in the same week. Something that only cold sends depend on did. That is a deliverability question before it is a copy question, and the deliverability team capped sends for a reason nobody on the marketing team has asked about.

## Sharper constraint

The brand voice page in Notion was last edited before the new SDR joined, and the two cold sequences that fell furthest were both rewritten by the new SDR in the last six weeks, with legal copied but the tone read skipped because nobody owns it. So there are two problems, not one. A pair that says "it is all deliverability" is half right, and a pair that says "rewrite the copy" is also half right. The edge is knowing there are two.

## Joint stand

Reconcile your two Friday hypotheses into one sentence you both sign, which will have to name both problems.

Why this is a real edge: pairs holding the merged exhibit will ask the facilitator about deliverability in the first ten minutes. Everyone else finds out at Exhibit 2, thirty minutes later.
