# The Sequence Nobody Owns

Marcus Bell manages the eight person marketing team at Northwind Logistics Software. The team's twelve outbound email sequences in HubSpot went from a 4 percent reply rate to 1.8 percent over two months and nobody can say why. At the end of the month Marcus presents a fix to the CRO: rewrite all twelve, or change how sequences are reviewed. He has not decided. What you need to know about how sequences are written, reviewed, and measured, and what he has already ruled out, you will earn this week.
