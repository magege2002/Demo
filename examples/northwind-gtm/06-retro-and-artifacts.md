# Retro and artifacts, The Sequence Nobody Owns

## Retro, fifteen minutes

What worked. What did not. What changes for the next event. Marcus answers last.

## Reasoning card, one per pair

Result: the stand in one sentence. Reasoning: three to five sentences in the pair's words, including what they believed before Exhibit 2. Seed: the Copilot prompt they used to check a sequence against the brand voice page, written so the next person edits it rather than copies it.

## Revised SOP skeleton, outbound sequence QA

1. Writer drafts or edits the sequence in HubSpot and posts it in the marketing Slack channel with the audience named (cold, warm, re-engage).
2. Copilot first pass, run by the writer: compare against the Notion brand voice page, list tone issues, and flag any sentence that claims a delivery time or a saving.
3. The review owner (one named person, with a named backup) reads the flagged items and the Copilot notes and signs or sends back. Nothing cold goes out without this signature.
4. Any flagged claim goes to legal with the source attached. No source, no claim.
5. Every other Friday the review owner, not Marcus, updates the reply and bounce sheet from HubSpot, by audience, and posts the two numbers.
6. New joiners run steps 1 and 2 with the review owner on their first three sequences.

Human checkpoints: steps 3, 4, and 6. Owner: Marcus names one this week.

## The delta

The intake's process had no owner for the review step, a tone check that could be skipped, and a measurement that lived with the manager every other Friday. The revised process names an owner, puts an AI first pass in front of the human read so the human reads flags instead of everything, routes claims to legal with a source, and moves the measurement to the owner by audience so a domain problem shows up as a cold-only fall the week it happens. The change the manager can hold is bounce rate and reply rate by audience on the next four Fridays.
